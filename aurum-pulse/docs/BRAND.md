# AURUM PULSE — Official working design system (pre-trademark clearance)

**Brand name:** AURUM PULSE by Genesis. **Tagline:** Find your frequency. **Positioning:** your personal music home, equalizer, discovery system and connected listening infrastructure; designed to be expressive, trustworthy, independent and audibly good.

## Brand architecture
- **AURUM**: parent music identity, preserved from the existing native Genesis Player Android source and older BRKN waves prototypes.
- **PULSE**: flagship unified connected player, web command surface and mobile listening experience.
- **AURUM Gold**: protected, owner-approved canonical playlist, no algorithmic additions.
- **AURUM Discover / Apollo**: next-track discovery, explainable recommendations and auditions.
- **AURUM Sound Lab**: live EQ/audio controls, verified bypass and route-specific safety.
- **AURUM Vault**: private files, catalog, backups, explicit rights provenance.
- **AURUM Connect**: OpenSubsonic, MCP and permitted integrations; capabilities bounded by real identity and consent.

## Color and type
- Obsidian `#080B17`, midnight panel `#121727`, pearl `#F9F8FF`, muted steel `#A0AAC3`.
- Signature: solar gold `#FFCB70`, flash coral `#FF9C81`, pulse magenta `#F663B4`, neon violet `#A862FA`, electric cyan `#48E4FF`.
- Fonts: system/Inter-compatible sans. Do not bundle proprietary font files.
- Motion: waves respond to *actual playback*. Reduce/disable motion for OS accessibility preference. No misleading animations that imply music is playing.
- Icon: custom abstract A/pulse mark. Lockup in `web/assets/aurum-pulse-lockup.svg`; icon in `web/assets/aurum-pulse.svg`.

## Voice and onboarding
Warm, confident, music-loving, useful and sincere. Never shame preferences or invent music access. Three steps: introduction, choose moods, optional local identity/privacy. Later production step 4: connect authorized library/account, user consent and privacy export.
